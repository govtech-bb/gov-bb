Use this service to apply for a licence to operate a restaurant. You can use it for a new licence or to renew one you already hold.

If your restaurant only operates for a short time — for example, a stall, booth or bar at an event running for up to 30 days — apply for a [licence to operate a temporary restaurant](/business-trade/apply-for-temporary-restaurant-licence) instead.

## What you will need

-   Your name, address, phone number and email address
-   The name and address of the restaurant
-   The name and contact details of the owner or operator, if that is not you

You do not need to upload any documents to apply. If you already have documents about the premises, such as plans, you can upload them with your application.

<a data-start-link>Start now</a>

## What happens next

-   Your application goes to the Environmental Health Department at the polyclinic serving the parish where the restaurant is.
-   An environmental health officer may arrange to inspect the restaurant before a licence is issued.
-   They will contact you using the details you gave.
