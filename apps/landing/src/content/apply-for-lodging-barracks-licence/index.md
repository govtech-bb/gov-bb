Use this service to register for the first time or renew the registration for:

- a **lodging house**, a place where people pay to stay for a short time and may share a sleeping room with other people
- **barracks**, a property with separate rooms where people share facilities such as bathrooms or toilets

This service is not for hotels, guest houses or inns.

You can read the [Health Services (Lodging Houses and Barracks) Regulations, 1970](https://oag.gov.bb/attachments/Health%20Services%20(Lodging%20Houses%20and%20Barracks)%20Regulations,%201970%20Cap44'R.PDF) for the full legal requirements.

## Before you start

You may need:

- your current registration number, if you are renewing
- the number and size of the rooms
- the number of people who can stay at the property
- the number of beds and bunk beds
- the number of toilets and showers
- the type of lighting used at the property
- if you are registering for the first time, a site plan of the property or your Planning and Development Department application number

Planning and Development used to be called Town and Country Planning.

## When to apply

Your registration expires on December 31 each year.

Renew it by the first business day in January.

## Complete the form

There are two ways to register.

1. ### Apply online

   Allow about **10 to 15 minutes** to complete the form.

   <a data-start-link>Start now</a>

2. ### Apply using a paper form

   You can get a paper application from a polyclinic.

   Complete the form and return it to the polyclinic for the area where the lodging house or barracks is.

## Cost

There is no cost to register or to renew a registration.

## What happens after you apply

- Your application goes to the Environmental Health Department for the area where the property is.
- They will review it and may contact you if they need more information.
- The property will be inspected before it is registered.
- If your application is approved, you will receive a confirmation email and the Environment Health Office will mail your registration. You can also ask to collect it from your assigned polyclinic.

Submitting an application does not mean that the lodging house or barracks has been registered.
