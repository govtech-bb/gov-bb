## Before you start

Use this service to apply for a restaurant licence from Environmental Health.

### What you will need

You will need:

- the restaurant name and address
- details about the food and drink you plan to sell
- details about where food will be prepared and stored
- information about water, handwashing and waste
- details about what you will use to cook, store or keep food hot or cold

### How long it takes

Allow about 15 minutes to complete the form.

## What happens next

Environmental Health will review your application.

They may need to inspect the restaurant before they decide.

They will contact you with the result. If your application is approved, they will tell you what to do next to get your licence.
