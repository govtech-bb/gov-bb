Use this service to apply for or renew a Food Business Licence from Environmental Health.

## Who this licence is for

You may need a Food Business Licence if you sell or provide food and customers do not usually sit and eat at the business.

This includes:

- canteens
- shops selling food
- food businesses operating from home
- food trucks
- mobile food vendors
- food carts, such as burger or hot dog carts
- snow cone vendors
- coconut vendors

Restaurants, caterers and bakers have their own applications. If you start this application and Environmental Health finds that you need one of those instead, they will tell you and move you to the right process.

## Before you start

### New businesses

You will need a layout drawn to scale, showing the size of the different areas and where they are.

If the business is mobile, the layout should show the full setup, including the vehicle or cart if one is used.

You can upload the layout with your application or provide it separately to Environmental Health.

### Renewing your licence

You only need a new layout if changes to the business affect the layout.

A Food Business Licence lasts for one year from the date it is issued. You need to renew it each year.

## What you will need

You may need:

- your current Food Business Licence number, if you are renewing
- the Barbados National Identification (ID) number for individual owners
- the business registration number for any company owners
- a vehicle registration number, if the business uses a vehicle
- details about other places where food or drink is prepared
- details about your food suppliers
- a layout drawn to scale, if you need to provide one
- medical certificates, if you want to upload them now

All staff who handle food or serve customers need a valid medical certificate. You can upload the certificates now or provide them at the inspection.

## How long it takes

Allow about 15 minutes to complete the form.

## What happens after you apply

Environmental Health will review your application and contact you to arrange an inspection.

They must inspect the food business before they can issue a licence. They may need to inspect it more than once.

If the inspection finds a problem, Environmental Health will tell you what needs to be fixed and how long you have to fix it.

If your application is approved, Environmental Health will tell you what happens next. Your licence may be mailed to you or collected.
