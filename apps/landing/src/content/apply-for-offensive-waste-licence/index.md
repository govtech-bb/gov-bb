Use this service to apply for or renew an offensive trade licence.

“Offensive trade” is the legal name for business activities that may cause fumes, gases, waste or strong smells.

You need this licence if your business does any of the following:

-   heat animal blood or organs
-   heat or crush animal bones
-   take wool off sheep skins
-   clean animal guts
-   make string from animal guts
-   kill animals for food or other uses
-   melt animal fat
-   make leather from animal skins
-   make chemicals or acids
-   make glue
-   make fertiliser from animal waste
-   make soap by boiling fats or oils

You must have a current licence to do this work.

You must apply for yourself.

## Before you apply

-   The licence expires on December 31st each year.
-   You cannot transfer the licence to another person or place.
-   Apply for a new licence if the address where you do the work has changed.

## What you will need

You will need plans and details for the building if you are:

-   applying for a new licence
-   renewing and have made or plan to make changes to the building

## How long it takes

The form takes about 10 minutes to complete. It may take longer if you need to upload plans.

## What happens after you apply

Environmental Health will review your application.

A Medical Officer of Health will decide whether to approve your vehicle.

Environmental Health will tell you the result.
