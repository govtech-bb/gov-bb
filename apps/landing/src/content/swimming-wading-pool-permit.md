Every swimming and wading pool needs a permit from Environmental Health.

You can apply for a permit for the current year or the next year.

The permit runs from January 1st to December 31st of the year you apply for.

## What counts as a swimming or wading pool

### Swimming pool

Definition to be confirmed by Environmental Health.

### Wading pool

Definition to be confirmed by Environmental Health.

If you are applying for someone else, you must be authorised to apply.

## Before you start

You may need to check how much water the pool can hold, in gallons or cubic metres.

## How long it takes

Allow about 5 minutes to complete the application.

## What happens after you apply

Environmental Health will review your application.

They may contact you if they need more information or need to inspect the pool.

They will let you know the outcome and what you need to do next.
