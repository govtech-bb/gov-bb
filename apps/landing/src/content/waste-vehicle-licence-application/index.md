Use this service to apply for or renew a licence to move human waste, rotting animal or plant waste, or other waste that could harm people’s health.

The law calls this “offensive matter”. You need this licence if you move this type of waste in Barbados.

Your licence expires on December 31st each year.

## Before you start

A Medical Officer of Health must approve the vehicle before you use it.

It must be strong, leakproof and covered. Waste and smells must not escape.

Human waste may only be moved from 10pm to 6am unless a Medical Officer of Health approves another time.

## What you will need

You may need:

-   the current licence number, if this is a renewal
-   the vehicle registration number

## Cost

There is no cost for this service.

## How long it takes

The form takes about 10 minutes to complete.

## What happens after you apply

Environmental Health will review your application.

A Medical Officer of Health will decide whether to approve your vehicle.

Environmental Health will tell you the result.

## Contact us

If you need help, contact the Ministry of Health and Wellness. Full contact details are on the offensive matter licence.
