Use this service to apply for a new hotel licence or renew an existing licence.

## What you need before you start

You need:

- the hotel's name and address
- the maximum number of guests who can stay at the hotel at one time
- the number of bedrooms, occupants, water closets, baths and basins on each floor, and how many rooms have their own water closet, bath or basin
- the number of male and female staff, and the changing rooms, lockers, hand wash basins and water closets provided for them
- the operator's name, phone number and email address, if you are not the operator
- your current Hotel Licence number, if you are renewing a licence
- a site plan of the hotel, if you are applying for a new licence

You give the floor details one floor at a time, so it helps to have them written down before you start.

You can upload the site plan as a PDF, JPG or PNG. Each file must be 5 MB or smaller.

<a data-start-link>Start now</a>

## What happens after you apply

Your application goes to the Environmental Health Department at your local polyclinic. They will review it and may contact you if they need more information.

Submitting an application does not mean that a Hotel Licence has been granted.
