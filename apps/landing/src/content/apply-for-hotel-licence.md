## About this service

Use this service to apply for a new Hotel Licence or renew an existing licence.

It should take about 5 minutes to complete the form.

## Before you start

You may need to check:

- the maximum number of guests who can stay at the hotel at one time
- the current Hotel Licence number, if you are renewing a licence

## What happens after you apply

Environmental Health will review your application. They may contact you if they need more information.

Submitting an application does not mean that a Hotel Licence has been granted.
