Use this service to ask for a food safety check, apply for approval for a temporary food or drink setup, or apply for approval for a restaurant or food business.

You can apply for more than one at the same time.

## Before you start

It may help to have:

- the address of the event, temporary setup or business
- details of the food and drink you plan to prepare, sell or serve
- the address of anywhere else food or drink will be prepared
- an Environmental Health reference number, if you already have one
- documents such as a site or setup plan, a licence, or a medical certificate (you may not need all of these)

## What do you need to do?

You can select more than one option.

☐ I need a food safety check

Ask Environmental Health to check how food or drink is prepared, stored, handled or served.

☐ I need approval for a temporary food or drink setup

For a stall, pop-up, home-based setup, mobile or roadside setup, or a setup at an event. The temporary setup must run for 30 days or less.

☐ I need approval for a restaurant or food business

For an ongoing business that prepares, sells or supplies food or drink. You do not need to know which licence applies. We will work this out from your answers.
