## Before you start

Use this service to apply for a new funeral establishment licence or to renew your licence.

You can apply if you are the owner of a funeral establishment.

You will need:

- your full name and address
- the name and address of your establishment
- the dimensions of your preparation room
- a list of embalmers who work at your establishment, split by male and female
- proof that all embalmers have current licences (these will be validated)

If you are renewing your licence, you can apply from [DATE — to be confirmed] before your current licence expires on 31 December.

## What happens after you apply

Your application will be inspected. The inspection checks:

- cleaning and sanitation of equipment and premises
- ventilation

If your establishment meets the requirements, your application will be approved by the Health Officer, Senior Officer, and Principal Officer, then reviewed by the Medical Officer of Health.

If inspection finds issues, you will be contacted about how to proceed.

## Cost

There is no fee for this application.
