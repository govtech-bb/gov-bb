## Who is this licence for

Owners of a funeral establishment needing to obtain or renew a funeral establishment licence.

## Before you start

Use this service to apply for a new funeral establishment licence or to renew your licence.

You will need:

-   the dimensions of your preparation room
-   maximum number of bodies that can be stored
-   a list of embalmers who work at your establishment, split by male and female
-   proof that all embalmers and directors have current licences (these will be validated)

## When to apply

### New Businesses

When you are opening a new funeral establishment, you must apply for this licence.

### Renewing your licence

Your licence lasts for one year. It expires one year after licence issuance. This expiration date is also included on your licence for your reference. Apply to renew it at least 30 days before it expires.

## Cost

There is neither a cost to apply, nor to receive your licence.

## How long does it take?

Allow about 15 - 20 minutes to complete the form.

## How to apply for a funeral establishment licence

1\. **Request a licence online**  

<a data-start-link>Start now</a>

2\. **Get a paper application for a funeral establishment licence form from your nearest polyclinic**  
You must complete it by hand and supply the relevant supporting documents to the polyclinic within the catchment area of the establishment

## What happens after you apply

Your application will be inspected. The inspection will check your:

-   processes for the cleaning and sanitation of equipment and premises
-   ventilation

If your establishment meets the requirements, your application will be approved by the Health Officer, Senior Officer, and Principal Officer, then reviewed by the Medical Officer of Health.

If inspection finds issues, you will be contacted about how to proceed.
