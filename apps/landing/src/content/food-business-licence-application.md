Use this service to apply for or renew a food business licence from Environmental Health.

## Before you apply

### What you will need

You may need:

- the food business name and address
- the type of food business you plan to run
- details about the food and drink you plan to sell or prepare
- details about where food will be prepared
- information about how food will be stored, kept hot or kept cold, if this applies to your business

### How long it takes

Allow about 15 minutes to complete the form.

## What happens after you apply

Environmental Health will review your application.

They may contact you if they need more information or need to arrange an inspection.

They will contact you with the result.

If they approve your application, they will tell you what to do next to get your licence.
